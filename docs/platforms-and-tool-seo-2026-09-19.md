## Search-intent review

Searches reviewed the six calculator themes plus Shopify CRO and AI marketing workflow services. Findings are qualitative query/intent observations, not search-volume, competition-score or rank measurements.

| Tool | Query focus | Useful distinction in MLabs |
|---|---|---|
| RTO | RTO cost calculator, COD return-to-origin cost | Separate COD/prepaid rates and direct cost from revenue at risk. |
| Unit economics | ecommerce unit economics calculator, contribution margin per order | Show actual cost assumptions and explain blended ad cost per order. |
| Bundles | product bundle pricing calculator, bundle discount margin | Support different product prices and disclose adoption/uplift assumptions. |
| Marketing | ecommerce marketing budget calculator, channel ROAS plan | Model spend allocation and channel returns; do not imply revenue-target reverse budgeting is implemented. |
| Runway | cash runway calculator, burn rate calculator | Explain the 24-month model and distinction between first-month burn and future cash balances. |
| Inventory | reorder point calculator, safety stock | Explain safety days versus units, and trigger stock level versus reorder quantity. |

Service results emphasise problem-specific scope and implementation evidence. Existing pages already cover deliverables, fit, inputs, price and human handoff. Do not make duplicate keyword pages or add unsupported success statistics to imitate competitors.
